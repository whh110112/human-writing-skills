# Capability and Power-State Continuity

## Aim

Keep combat power, professional competence, authority, abilities, equipment,
resources, injuries, cooldowns, costs, and counters consistent across long fiction.
Judge outcomes from the active state and matchup, not from a single rank label.

## Activation Gate

Use only when the task, draft, or supplied continuity material establishes capability,
power, rank, equipment, resource, injury, authority, training, or system-rule constraints.
In audit workflows select `capability`; automatic selection also requires supplied
context. Do not load for ordinary dialogue or domestic scenes without such constraints.

## State Card

For each relevant actor, extract:

```text
baseline rank/competence and evidence location
usable abilities, permissions, equipment, allies, and resources
current injuries, fatigue, cooldowns, debts, ammunition, and environmental limits
known counters, matchup advantages, uncertainty, and hidden information
latest earned change and its on-page gate
```

Separate permanent baseline from temporary state. Separate public reputation, a
character's belief, and narrator-confirmed capability.

## Transition Ledger

Every meaningful change needs a traceable transition:

```text
prior state -> trigger/training/acquisition/loss -> cost or constraint
-> new state -> first demonstrated consequence
```

For a surprising result, test the full matchup:

```text
goal -> available action -> resistance/counter -> information advantage
-> environment/teamwork/luck -> paid cost -> result
```

A lower-ranked actor may win through a seeded counter, preparation, terrain,
deception, sacrifice, temporary aid, or opponent impairment. A higher rank alone does
not guarantee victory. The error is an outcome or state change with no compatible gate.

## Audit

- Flag forgotten injuries, depleted resources, cooldowns, costs, permissions, or limits.
- Flag abilities, equipment, authority, or knowledge that appear only to solve the scene.
- Flag rank, title, form, skill, or resource changes without a transition.
- Flag an outcome that contradicts the established matchup without preparation,
  counter, environmental cause, error, uncertainty, or paid cost.
- Flag old retrieved fragments that silently override a newer canonical ledger state.
- Treat boasts, rumors, mistaken beliefs, and unreliable narration as claims, not facts.
