# Chapter Pattern Audit

## Aim

Detect repeated chapter architecture that makes a serial feel machine-produced even
when individual sentences vary.

## Activation Gate

Use only for three or more chapters, a chapter batch, or an explicit request to check
structural repetition. Do not infer recurrence from one chapter.

## Chapter Fingerprint

Compress each chapter into:

```text
entry mode -> first pressure -> main transaction -> turn -> emotional crest
-> exit mode or hook
```

Also record dominant paragraph cadence, exposition placement, and whether the chapter
opens by rebuilding time, weather, location, appearance, and mood.

## Audit

- Compare functions, not repeated words alone.
- Flag three or more chapters that use substantially the same ordered architecture
  without a genre reason or escalating variation.
- Flag repeated resets, repeated arrival-and-briefing scenes, identical reveal timing,
  copied emotional arcs, and unrelated shock hooks used as substitutes for movement.
- Do not flag deliberate ritual, procedural form, running jokes, mirrored chapters,
  mystery clue patterns, or formal experimentation when repetition accumulates meaning.
- Distinguish a recurring series grammar from a copied chapter template.

## Repair

Change the earliest repeated function that does not carry new pressure. Vary access,
viewpoint, starting residue, obstacle timing, decision ownership, or exit consequence.
Do not randomize structure merely to avoid similarity.
