# Whole-Book Story Architecture Audit

## Activation Gate

Use only for an explicitly requested whole-book review of fiction or webnovels with
at least three identifiable chapters. Read the completed chunk reports and the
canonical outline before this pass. Do not load during ordinary chapter drafting,
single-chapter review, or serious-document work.

## Review Contract

Build a compact map for every chapter: protagonist decision, obstacle, changed
relationship or state, cost, time movement, and exit pressure. Cite chapter and
body evidence for each entry. A quiet or transitional chapter may legitimately
have no dramatic reversal; record its function rather than inventing one.

Across the book, ask four separate questions:

1. Do consequential choices alter later possibilities, or does the plot restore
   the same state after each chapter?
2. Does conflict change in kind, cost, or knowledge, or merely repeat the same
   obstacle and resolution with new scenery?
3. Are time jumps and viewpoint shifts intelligible and useful, including
   deliberate discontinuity, or are they accidental resets?
4. Do morally difficult choices remain genuinely open where intended, or does
   the narrator immediately explain and settle every implication?

Compare chapter functions, not sentence patterns alone. Distinguish a deliberate
serial rhythm, genre convention, ritual, or motif from accidental single-track
repetition. Do not inject ambiguity, conflict, or nonlinear chronology merely to
make a story appear less machine-generated.

## Evidence And Handoff

For a suspected defect, identify at least two relevant chapters, the smallest
structural revision, its continuity cost, and which neighboring chunks require
re-audit. If the chunk reports do not contain enough evidence, mark the point
unverified and request the exact source span. Never claim a whole-book pass is
complete from a partial set of reports.

Return a chapter-function table, confirmed cross-chapter patterns, deliberate
patterns to retain, unresolved evidence requests, and a ranked repair sequence.
