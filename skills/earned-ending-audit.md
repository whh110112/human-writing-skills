# Earned Ending Audit

## Aim

Stop a scene, chapter, story, article, or report at its last useful consequence instead
of appending a polished bookend that only explains how the reader should feel. Preserve
real reflection and quiet endings when they change meaning; remove ornamental closure.

## Activation

Use for scene or chapter generation, explicit ending review, deep humanization, or an
AI-trace audit. It applies across languages and genres, but the exit contract changes by
document type. Do not force suspense, action, ambiguity, or a picturesque final image
into every ending.

## Last Meaningful Change Test

Locate the last sentence or beat where something became newly true:

- action, position, possession, access, or material state changed
- a character chose, refused, understood, misread, revealed, or withheld something
- knowledge, leverage, risk, obligation, intimacy, or relationship boundaries changed
- a verified fact, limitation, attribution, decision, deadline, or next step was added

Test every sentence after that point. Keep it only when it sharpens the consequence,
hands pressure to what follows, changes the meaning of a concrete detail, or fulfills a
required document function. If deleting the tail loses no event, inference boundary,
character decision, evidence, or intentional tonal turn, the scene probably ended earlier.

## Reflective Bookend Families

Flag a terminal cluster by function, not by one forbidden phrase:

- silent embrace, shared stillness, or looking outward after the interaction has landed
- sunset, moving light, rain, traffic, night, or passing time used as automatic dissolve
- a character who reflects, realizes, wonders what the future holds, or defines life/love
- `as if`, `仿佛`, `像是`, or equivalent language that translates the scene into a theme
- lost time being “made up,” wounds being “healed,” a “new beginning,” fate, hope, tomorrow,
  or another generic promise not created by a specific choice
- narrator forecasts such as “everything was about to change” without a new event
- a final sentence that restates the emotional meaning already shown by the prior action

One element can be valid. The warning sign is a low-information terminal stack or the
same ending function recurring across scenes.

## Fiction And Webnovel Exit Contract

A scene ending may land on:

- a changed action, object, distance, permission, or physical condition
- a decision with a foreseeable cost
- an interruption, arrival, discovery, refusal, or unanswered pressure
- a quiet image whose meaning changed because of the scene
- deliberate stillness that creates tension, intimacy, estrangement, or a new boundary
- an earned reflection that changes the viewpoint character's next action or interpretation

Reflection is not progress merely because it sounds thoughtful. Do not explain an image
after it has already carried the emotion. Do not append scenery because the requested
generation length has ended. Before delivery, compare the final three sentences with
the last concrete change and run a deletion test. Often the correct repair is to stop
one or two sentences earlier.

For chapter endings, leave residue that the next chapter can inherit. A hook must grow
from the scene's own change; do not replace a reflective bookend with a random threat,
question, slammed door, or prophetic sentence.

## News And Feature Exit Contract

- Hard news may end at the last useful verified fact, attributed response, constraint,
  background item, or dated next step. It does not need a moral or optimistic summary.
- A feature kicker may return to a person, object, place, or quotation only when the
  return adds consequence or altered meaning. A decorative quote or scenic dissolve is
  not stronger merely because it occupies the last line.
- Do not claim that an event symbolizes a broader era, proves resilience, or points to
  a hopeful future unless reporting and attribution support that conclusion.

## Academic, Technical, And Official Exit Contract

- Academic conclusions synthesize supported findings, limits, and implications at the
  same claim strength as the evidence. They may summarize because the genre requires it,
  but should not repeat the abstract, inflate significance, or bolt on generic future work.
- Technical endings state verified status, remaining limitation, operating condition,
  unresolved risk, or next test.
- Official documents end with the applicable decision, responsible actor, deadline,
  effective scope, contact, or attachment convention. Do not add ceremonial uplift that
  changes no duty or decision.

## Audit And Repair

1. Mark the last meaningful change and the proposed stopping point.
2. Classify the tail: consequence, handoff, changed image, required close, summary echo,
   scenic dissolve, theme caption, future promise, or default kicker.
3. Compare nearby scene or section endings for repeated function and cadence.
4. Run the deletion test before rewriting. If context is needed, replace the tail with
   the smallest consequence, decision, limitation, or ledger residue.
5. Re-read the next opening. It must inherit the retained ending rather than reset it.

Do not replace one stock ending with another. Vary exit type according to what the scene
or document actually changed.
