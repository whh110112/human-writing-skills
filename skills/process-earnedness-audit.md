# Process Earnedness Audit

## Aim

Catch scenes that announce an interesting task, skip the task itself, and jump to a
result while spending their words on atmosphere, generic expertise, or retrospective
summary. Make outcomes feel caused, resisted, and earned.

## Activation Gate

Use for scenes centered on a consequential process such as investigation, research,
negotiation, diagnosis, construction, training, combat, craft, administration, or
another domain task. Do not load merely because a character has a profession.

## Earned Outcome Chain

Map the process as:

```text
promise -> attempt -> resistance -> judgment or skill -> cost/evidence -> result
```

Not every scene needs all six beats on the page. A skipped beat is acceptable when
the genre, viewpoint, pacing, or prior chapter has already earned it. The test is
whether the reader can understand why this result occurred and what made it specific
to these people and constraints.

## Audit

- Locate the process promised by the premise, chapter title, setup, or character goal.
- Mark where the draft compresses an attempt into labels such as "after some effort,"
  "through expert analysis," or "the talks went well."
- Check whether expertise appears through choices, questions, corrections, tradeoffs,
  failed attempts, or use of evidence rather than praise from the narrator.
- Check whether resistance changes the plan instead of merely delaying it.
- Check whether success consumes time, information, money, status, safety, trust, or
  another relevant resource.
- Flag detailed setup followed by an unearned result, and detailed atmosphere that
  displaces the process readers were promised.
- Preserve deliberate ellipsis when the omitted procedure is repetitive, irrelevant,
  sensitive, or outside the viewpoint's competence.

## Repair

Restore the earliest missing causal beat. Usually one consequential choice, failed
attempt, revealed constraint, or paid cost is stronger than several paragraphs of
technical exposition.

```text
- Earned outcome chain:

- Expertise evidence present or absent:
- Valid reason for ellipsis:
- Smallest beat to restore:
```
